About Haven

Experience matters. But so does how you use it.

After more than 20 years in physiotherapy, I've learned that experience alone isn't what makes treatment effective. What matters is how we apply our knowledge, question assumptions and make decisions when a problem isn't straightforward.

At Haven, my approach begins with understanding the individual, looking beyond the immediate symptoms and considering the factors contributing to their pain or movement difficulties.

Rather than relying on a standard treatment formula, I use clinical reasoning, careful assessment and a range of treatment options to develop an approach suited to each person's presentation and response.

Experience & Clinical Reasoning

Understanding the problem before deciding how best to treat it.

Targeted, Hands-On Treatment

Selecting appropriate treatment techniques based on clinical findings, rather than following a standard formula.

Individualised Care

Adapting treatment to each person's presentation, response and progress.

A considered approach to physiotherapy

Good physiotherapy involves more than identifying where something hurts. It requires understanding why the problem has developed, what may be maintaining it and which treatment options are most appropriate.

My focus is on combining experience, clinical reasoning and hands-on treatment to help people make meaningful progress, particularly when their symptoms are persistent, complex or haven't responded as expected to previous treatment.

The People Behind Haven

Philip Wulff Physiotherapist | Master of Physiotherapy Studies Over 20 years of experience in musculoskeletal physiotherapy. Read moreRead less

Phil graduated from the University of Queensland in 2005 and has more than 20 years of clinical experience across sporting and private practice settings. His particular interests include musculoskeletal injuries, back and neck pain, soft tissue treatment and understanding the mechanics of movement. He brings this experience to Haven through careful assessment, clinical reasoning and hands-on treatment.
