About Haven

A small practice: one physiotherapist, one practice manager, and a long connection with the Central Coast.

Philip Wulff

Physiotherapist, Master of Physiotherapy Studies

Phil qualified as a physiotherapist from the University of Queensland in 2005. He has worked in a range of sporting and clinical environments in Brisbane and on the Central Coast. He has a special interest in sporting injuries, back and neck pain, soft tissue therapy and helping people understand the underlying mechanics of the way we move well.

Phil has a desire to help people of all ages and applies his knowledge of assessment, diagnosis and treatment to a range of complex conditions, especially those involving the shoulder and neck, the back, hip and pelvis, and the upper and lower limbs.

Since moving to Terrigal in 2009, Phil has built a good working relationship within many circles of the Central Coast community.

Sports Phil treats

Phil has an avid interest in sports, and he treats many members of the local sporting groups, including:

  • Runners. Running groups are great for social interaction, but running injuries are common.
  • Brazilian Jiu Jitsu.
  • Soccer players of all ages, and especially the over-35s and over-45s, who tend to keep us busy.
  • Surfing, cricket, rugby, tennis, basketball, Oztag and dragon-boating.
  • Golfers. Phil once played off a 9 handicap, before having children and running his business.
  • Gymnastics and dancers.

Essentially, we are always interested in sports.

Belinda Lazzaroni

Practice Manager

Belinda brings with her a strong organisational background and the skills required to coordinate a growing practice.
