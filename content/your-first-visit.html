Your first visit. Know what to expect.

A little preparation goes a long way. Here's what your first appointment at Haven looks like, from arriving at the clinic to taking your first steps towards feeling better.

{! The four parts of a first visit, always open: the numeral and its line icon sit in a narrow left column from 840px, with a hairline between one part and the next. The numerals take the readable orange, as the booking steps on the contact page do. !}

  1. More time to understand your problem.

    Your first appointment is longer than a standard follow-up, giving us time to explore your symptoms, relevant history, previous treatments and what you're hoping to achieve. We combine this conversation with a thorough clinical assessment to better understand what's contributing to your problem and identify the most appropriate treatment approach.

  2. Your own private treatment room.

    At Haven, your appointment takes place in a private, enclosed treatment room, not an open studio or curtained treatment area. You'll have dedicated one-on-one time with your physiotherapist, with the privacy to discuss your concerns openly and the attention needed for individualised care.

  3. Assessment, treatment and reassessment.

    Wherever appropriate, we aim to begin treatment during your first visit. This might involve targeted hands-on therapy, dry needling, laser or shockwave treatment, depending on what your assessment reveals. Our goal is to give you an early experience of how treatment can help, while establishing a clear direction for your recovery.

    Where possible, we'll also reassess your movement, symptoms or tolerance after treatment. This helps us understand your response and refine our approach.

  4. A clear direction for what comes next.

    We'll discuss our findings, explain the initial treatment priorities and provide practical advice relevant to your situation. That might include activity modifications, simple exercises or recommendations for further investigation. Your next steps will reflect what we've learned during your visit, rather than a predetermined treatment schedule.
