Treatments & Technology

{! Three phrases with the middle one in Haven's orange. They run on as one sentence on a narrow screen and balance themselves; each takes its own line from 640px. !}

Great treatment starts long before any treatment is applied.

It starts with high standards: listening carefully, observing closely, asking the right questions and understanding what may be contributing to your symptoms.

At Haven, every appointment is dedicated one-on-one time with your physiotherapist. We invest in clinical knowledge, hands-on skills and advanced treatment technology because we believe every patient deserves our full attention and thoughtful, individualised care. The difference isn't just what treatments we offer. It's how we use them.

Our Approach

Reduce. Restore. Rebuild.

Treatment is guided by your symptoms, sensitivity, goals and response. These stages often overlap; they aren't a rigid formula.

{! The three stages take the home page's progress rule: the orange fills a third, two thirds and then the whole hairline as the stages advance. !}

  1. Reduce

    Ease pain, irritability, swelling and protective tension so movement and everyday activities become more comfortable.

  2. Restore

    Improve mobility, tissue tolerance and confidence through carefully selected treatment, movement and progressive loading.

  3. Rebuild

    Develop strength, capacity and more reliable movement patterns so improvements translate into everyday life, work and sport.

Treatment Options

We've invested in a carefully selected range of hands-on techniques and treatment technologies. Each has its strengths and purpose.

But having the right tools is only part of the picture. Knowing which to use, how to apply them and when to adjust treatment is where experience and clinical reasoning matter. Explore the options below and how they may work individually or together.

Hands-On Physiotherapy

Hands-on physiotherapy is at the heart of Haven's approach. Over more than 20 years, we've refined our palpation, trigger point therapy and manual treatment skills to identify and address areas of tension, sensitivity and restricted movement with exceptional attention to detail.

Our treatment combines targeted soft tissue and myofascial techniques, trigger point therapy and joint mobilisation or manipulation where appropriate. By carefully assessing how tissues feel, move and respond, we can direct treatment towards the areas most relevant to your symptoms.

It's a precise, individualised approach built on experience, clinical reasoning and the belief that how treatment is applied matters just as much as the technique itself.

Dry Needling

Dry needling is one of Haven's signature treatment techniques, refined through more than 20 years of hands-on clinical experience. Our approach draws on highly developed palpation and trigger point assessment skills to identify and precisely target sensitive muscles and areas of persistent tension.

By combining accurate needle placement with carefully selected techniques, we aim to reduce pain, release muscle tension and restore more comfortable movement. This targeted approach can be particularly valuable for stubborn muscle pain, deep-seated tightness and movement restrictions that have been difficult to resolve.

The difference lies in knowing where to treat, how to apply the technique and how to adapt treatment to achieve a meaningful response.

High-Power Laser Therapy

High-power laser therapy delivers targeted light energy to tissues, stimulating cellular processes involved in recovery and helping reduce pain and sensitivity. It can be particularly useful when tissues are highly sensitive, supporting more comfortable movement and greater tolerance for other treatments and exercise.

Radial Pressure Wave Therapy (RPW)

Radial pressure waves deliver mechanical pulses across a broader area of tissue, helping reduce pain and muscle tension while improving movement tolerance. RPW is particularly useful for selected muscle and tendon problems and can be used independently or combined with hands-on treatment, Focused Shockwave Therapy and exercise.

Focused Shockwave Therapy (FSWT)

Focused Shockwave Therapy is one of Haven's newest and most significant investments in advanced treatment technology. It delivers concentrated acoustic energy to a selected depth, allowing us to target specific tissues with precision. This expands our treatment options for persistent tendon and other musculoskeletal problems, particularly where a more focused approach is appropriate. We can combine FSWT with RPW, laser therapy and exercise to support progressive improvements in pain, movement and function.

Movement Assessment & Recovery Guidance

Movement tells us a great deal about how your body is functioning. At Haven, we assess how you move before treatment, identify restrictions or patterns contributing to discomfort, and reassess afterwards to understand what's changed.

When appropriate, we introduce simple, targeted exercises or modify existing activities to reinforce improvements, build confidence and help you move more comfortably. The emphasis is on choosing movements that suit your current capacity and support your recovery.

As your movement and tolerance improve, we can help guide your return to exercise, training, work or sport. Where more structured exercise rehabilitation or conditioning is needed, we encourage collaboration with exercise physiologists, trainers and other movement professionals.

Did you know?

Harder isn't always better.

Whether it's massage, shockwave therapy or exercise, more intensity doesn't automatically mean better results. Sometimes less is more. The aim is to apply enough treatment to encourage a useful response, then progress as your tolerance improves.
