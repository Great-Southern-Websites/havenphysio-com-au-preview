Treatments & Technology

Great treatment starts long before any treatment is applied.

It starts with high standards: listening carefully, observing closely, asking the right questions and understanding what may be contributing to your symptoms.

At Haven, we invest in clinical knowledge, hands-on skills and advanced treatment technology because we believe every patient deserves thoughtful, individualised care. The difference isn't just what treatments we offer. It's how we use them.

Our Approach

Reduce. Restore. Rebuild.

Treatment is guided by your symptoms, sensitivity, goals and response. These stages often overlap; they aren't a rigid formula.

{! The three stages take the home page's progress rule: the orange fills a third, two thirds and then the whole hairline as the stages advance. !}

  1. Reduce

    Ease pain, irritability, swelling and protective tension so movement and everyday activities become more comfortable.

  2. Restore

    Improve mobility, tissue tolerance and confidence through carefully selected treatment, movement and progressive loading.

  3. Rebuild

    Develop strength, capacity and more reliable movement patterns so improvements translate into everyday life, work and sport.
