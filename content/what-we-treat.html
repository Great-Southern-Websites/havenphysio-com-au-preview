What We Treat

Pain, stiffness or movement problems? Let's understand what's behind them.

Whether you're dealing with a recent injury, aching joints, persistent muscle tightness or a problem that keeps returning, the first step is understanding what's contributing to it. At Haven, we combine careful assessment, clinical reasoning and targeted treatment to help relieve pain, improve movement and support lasting progress.

Joint Pain, Stiffness & Arthritis

Stiff knees, aching hips, painful shoulders or increasingly restricted joints? Osteoarthritis and other joint problems can affect comfort, mobility and confidence. Sometimes discomfort isn't simply about the joint itself. Restricted movement, surrounding muscle tension, swelling or inflammation can contribute to pressure, aching and stiffness. Understanding these factors helps guide treatment to relieve symptoms and improve movement.

Did you know?

Inflammation, swelling and effusion are related, but don't mean quite the same thing. Inflammation is the body's response to irritation or injury. Swelling describes increased tissue volume, often due to fluid. Effusion refers to excess fluid within a joint or another body cavity. These terms commonly appear in medical reports.

Back, Neck & Spinal Pain

Back and neck pain can develop gradually or follow injury, prolonged positioning or changes in activity. Symptoms may include aching, stiffness, restricted movement or pain extending into the arms or legs. We assess spinal movement, surrounding muscles, joint function and contributing factors to guide treatment suited to your needs.

Muscle Pain & Tightness

Muscle pain isn't always simply a strained or overworked muscle. Persistent aching and tightness may involve muscle tension, fascial restrictions or other contributing factors. We assess muscle and surrounding tissue function, identifying tension, sensitivity and movement restrictions that may contribute to discomfort. Treatment aims to improve mobility and restore comfortable movement.

Tendon & Soft Tissue Problems

Tendon and soft tissue problems can cause pain during everyday activities, exercise or rest. Common presentations include tendinitis, bursitis, plantar fasciitis and irritation around muscles, tendons and joints. We assess tissue sensitivity, movement, loading and contributing factors to guide targeted treatment and improve comfort and function.

Sports & Everyday Injuries

Whether an injury occurs during sport, work or everyday activity, recovery involves understanding what happened and what may be limiting progress. We assess affected tissues, movement and functional demands to guide treatment and a practical return to usual activities, while helping reduce the risk of recurring problems.

Persistent & Complex Pain

When pain keeps returning, affects multiple areas or hasn't improved with previous treatment, it can be difficult to know what to try next. We review the broader picture, identify potentially contributing factors and develop a considered treatment plan, with realistic expectations and progress reviewed along the way.
