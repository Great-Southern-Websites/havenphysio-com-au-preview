The Haven Approach

Precision Diagnosis Understand the problem before choosing the treatment.

We take time to understand your symptoms, assess how you move and identify the likely causes of your pain or restriction. Detailed clinical assessment and reasoning help guide the most appropriate treatment strategy.

Targeted Treatment The right treatment, selected for the right reasons.

Treatment is selected according to your clinical findings and individual needs. This may include skilled hands-on therapy, specialised treatment technologies and rehabilitation strategies, rather than a routine or one-size-fits-all approach.

Better Outcomes Meaningful improvement in how you feel, move and live.

Our focus is on relieving pain, restoring movement and improving your ability to work, exercise and enjoy everyday life. Progress is reviewed and treatment adapted to support meaningful, lasting improvements.

The thinking behind Haven

Looking beyond the obvious.

After more than 20 years in physiotherapy, I've learned that understanding why a problem exists is often more important than simply treating where it hurts.

My approach combines detailed clinical assessment, fresh thinking and targeted treatment strategies, particularly when symptoms are complex, persistent or haven't responded as expected.

I look beyond the obvious, question assumptions and work to identify a clearer way forward.
